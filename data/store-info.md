# Fabric care

- **Wool-blend knits:** dry clean, or hand wash cold with a mild detergent. Never wring. Dry flat in shade — hanging stretches the shoulders. Store folded.
- **Cotton tees:** machine wash cold, inside out, with like colours. Tumble dry low or line dry. Wash printed tees inside out and do not iron directly over the print.
- **Ribbed tank:** cold machine wash, no bleach, low heat drying. Avoid fabric softener, it flattens the rib.
- Pilling on knitwear is normal in the first few wears; a fabric comb removes it without damage.
